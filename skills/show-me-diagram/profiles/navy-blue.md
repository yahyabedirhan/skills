# Profile: Navy Blue

Deep navy ground, cool blue lines and one warm gold accent. A calm dark look for slides and READMEs.

## Diagram types

Architecture, Layer stack, Sequence, Data flow, Deployment.

## Diagram style

Minimal dark.

## Look

Upstream density. Strokes 1.2 for emphasis. Gold only on the 1 or 2 focal nodes.
