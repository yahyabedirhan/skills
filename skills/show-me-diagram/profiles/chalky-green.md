# Profile: Chalky Green

Green chalkboard with chalk-white handwriting and a yellow chalk accent. For explaining a concept as in a classroom.

## Diagram types

Flowchart, Tree, Loop, Venn, Layer stack, Timeline.

## Diagram style

Minimal dark, with Sketchy.

## Look

Low density, at most 7 nodes. Strokes 1.4, corners 8. Yellow chalk only on the focal node.
